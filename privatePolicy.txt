In Short

NotifAlarm processes notification filters on your device. We do not track you.

Discord forwarding is optional and disabled by default. If you enable it,
matched notification titles and text, source app package names, filter names
and IDs, event IDs and timestamps are sent to the Discord channel configured
by your webhook. Discord processes those messages under its own privacy policy.
Choose filters carefully if notifications may contain personal information.

The Discord webhook is stored in the app's private storage and excluded from
Android backup and device transfer. You can disable forwarding or remove the
webhook in Settings.

The project is open source.
